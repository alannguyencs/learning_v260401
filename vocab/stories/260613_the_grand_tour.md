# The Grand Tour

It was 8:55 on Minh's first morning, and I met him down in the lobby of our huge, twelve-floor building. My only job that day was to show him around so he wouldn't get lost — because this place is huge, and everyone gets lost in their first week.

"Morning, Minh — welcome aboard! You found us alright? Great. Before we dive into any actual work, let me walk you through the building so you can get your bearings.

Right now we're in the lobby, on the ground floor. Over there, to your left, is the reception desk — if a courier or a visitor ever turns up for you, that's where they'll check in. Straight ahead, past those glass doors, are the turnstiles; you'll badge in with the access card I'll hand you in a minute.

Let's head up to our floor. We'll take the lift — sorry, the elevator, I keep switching — it's just around the corner, next to the stairwell. Our team sits on the seventh floor, so I'll hit the button for seven.

Once we're up there, you'll see it's an open-plan office. Our pod is at the far end, by the window. Your desk is the second one on the right, directly across from mine — so if you ever get stuck, just lean over and ask. Diagonally opposite us is the design team; don't worry about mixing them up, their corner is the one covered in sticky notes.

Now, a few landmarks you'll need day to day. The kitchenette — where the coffee lives — is down the hall, the first door on your left. Keep going past it and you'll reach the breakout area where we hold our stand-ups. The restrooms are opposite the kitchenette, hidden behind the printer.

If you ever need me and I'm not at my desk, I'm probably upstairs on the ninth floor — that's where the meeting rooms are. They're named after rivers, oddly. The big one, 'Mekong', is at the end of the corridor, on the corner. Bear in mind you have to book them in advance.

One more thing: the IT help desk — for laptop problems, badge problems, anything — is two floors down, on the fifth. Take the stairs if the lift is busy; they come out right next to the help desk anyway.

I think that covers the essentials. Take a moment to settle in, and whenever you're ready, swing by my desk and we'll get you set up on the systems. Don't hesitate to ask if anything's unclear — better to ask than to wander around lost. Sound good?"
