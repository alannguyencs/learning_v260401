# LLM Token Saving Strategies

The combined discipline of pulling all three proxy levers (caching + context compression + routing) **plus** prioritizing output-token reduction, to minimize cost and latency without hurting answer quality. *Example:* Put the smart receptionist, the short summaries, the cheap-vs-professor sorting, and the "keep answers short" rule together, and Alan's app gives the same quality answers for a fraction of the price — that whole playbook is the strategy.
