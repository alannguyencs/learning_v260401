## Story

Wei's daughter just started primary school and her teacher asked her to describe her room in English. Wei decided to help her practice by walking through their apartment together.

They started at the window. "See the **window sill**?" Wei pointed. "That's where we keep your **alarm clock** — the one that **rings** every morning at 7." His daughter touched the white **curtains** and pulled them open to let the sunlight in.

"My bed is very **comfortable**," she said, jumping onto it and hugging both **pillows**. Wei opened the **wardrobe** next to the bed. "This is where all your school uniforms go." Across from it stood a small **dresser** with four **drawers**. On top sat a **globe** — Wei spun it and asked, "Can you find Hong Kong?"

They moved to the desk area. "And this is your **wastebasket** — for used paper, not candy wrappers on the floor." A large **rug** lay **in the middle of** the room, soft enough for her to sit on while drawing.

Wei looked up. "What's that called?" he asked, pointing at the top of the room. "The **ceiling**!" she said. A lamp **hangs** from it. He pointed to the wall. "And this?" "The **light switch**!" She flipped it on and off three times. **Above** the switch, she had taped a **poster** of her favorite cartoon.

In the living room corner, Wei sat in his **armchair** and picked up the **remote control**. His wife had placed a **vase** with yellow flowers on the side table. "Now," Wei said, "can you describe your room to your teacher tomorrow?"

She nodded. She already knew all the words.
