## I. Money — Compound Growth Over Status Symbols

- **Saving is not investing (#1).** $5K/year into an S&P 500 index fund from your 20s becomes **$1.4M** at retirement. Two rules only: put it in an index fund, and stay in. Sandeep panic-sold in 2008 (lost 30%, refused to re-enter) — had he done nothing, his net worth today would be **40% higher**. Don't time the market, don't chase meme stocks/crypto/options.
- **Emergency fund (#8).** 3–6 months of expenses, untouched. A friend in his mid-40s pulled over with a numb face, got rushed to hospital, and was diagnosed with a brain tumour — no warning. Expect the unexpected.
- **Form your own FBI (#9) — Financial Bureau of Investigation.** Track where money goes, then categorise into **4 E's**: Emergency → Essentials → Equity → Enjoyment. Fund in that order. Only fund Enjoyment after the first three are covered. Use apps like Mint or Good Budget.
- **Kill status debt (#10).** US average credit card interest is **22–28%**. Designer clothes on credit make you look broke. Sandeep bought his first home at 36 with mortgage at 22% of annual expenses. Wealth = what you keep.
- **Financial literacy (#11).** Compound interest is the eighth wonder. Read *Psychology of Money* and *Algebra of Wealth*. Learn: compounding, credit scores (matter more than your dating profile), and how taxes work.
- **Automate everything (#12).** Savings/investment before paycheck hits, auto-pay every bill, guard-rails on discretionary spending. Let technology protect you from yourself.

## II. Career — Build Skills, Chase Equity, Move Where the Action Is

- **Stop following passion (#2).** A Canadian study: only **4%** of students can monetise their passion. People are passionate about art, music, photography — things that don't pay bills. Don't *find* passion — *build* it by becoming great at something monetisable.
- **Practice, practice, practice (#3).** Taylor Swift and Kobe didn't win on talent; Berklee musicians practise 8–10 hours/day until fingers bleed. Talent without practice is a Ferrari with no engine.
- **Career = investment portfolio (#4).** Sandeep started as a help-desk guy in a windowless Boston server room, smelling bad, getting yelled at — but logged **15,000 hours** in 5 years building skills *and* connections. Be a "learning chameleon" when mistreated. <20 years later he was CEO.
- **Move where the action is (#5).** You can't grow your network on Zoom in pyjamas. Sandeep regrets not moving to SF in the early 2000s; NYC was a game-changer when he did move. Exposure from collaborating face-to-face teaches what remote never will.
- **Equity over salary (#6).** The top 1% chase equity, not paychecks. Salary buys furniture; equity buys your future. 9/10 stock option grants miss — but one hit is a rainmaking event. Owning equity also shifts you to an owner mindset.
- **Get a mentor BEFORE 40 (#7).** Sandeep waited until 40 — mindfield with a blindfold. Once he got real mentors, he became CEO within 10 years. Avoid fake paid executive coaches — find real mentors invested in you. Read *Trillion Dollar Coach* (Bill Campbell).

## III. Mindset — Failure, Relationships, and H=O/D

- **Go analog (#13).** 1970s: 40% of high schoolers read ≥6 books/year. Today those numbers have flipped. A 2017 study: just a **one-week** social media detox improves attention and cognition. In a distracted world, focus is your superpower.
- **Failure is a teacher, not a tormentor (#14).** Fired three times, each time unemployed 6+ months. The most successful people fail the most. Learn or repeat.
- **Ask for help — fear rejection less (#15).** Ed Sheeran was rejected by every label (too ginger, too acoustic, too nerdy) — now sells out stadiums with Beyoncé. Success is a team sport. As Dumbledore said: help is given to those who ask.
- **Don't play the victim card (#16).** Sandeep is still friends with bosses who fired him. Business is never personal. Grudges only hurt you — the world moves on.
- **Partner choice is your most important decision (#17).** Wrong partner = emotional bankruptcy. Sandeep dated widely, spent 6–7 years with the right person before marrying in his 30s — still his best friend. Use **System 2** (slow, rational, long-term compatibility) — not **System 1** (fast, gut, infatuation) — when choosing.
- **Forgive your past (#18).** Sandeep's father was physically abusive; 8 pm doorbell = panic attack. Ran away, homeless on Mumbai train stations, then became a monk. Your past is a bag of bricks you can put down. Forgiveness is for you, not them.
- **True value is what you bring to others (#19).** Native American saying: "You live only as long as the last person who remembers you dies." Legacy = the lives you improve.
- **H = O/D (#20).** A senior monk's equation: Happiness = Own ÷ Desire. Desires are infinite; what you can own is finite. Manage the denominator. When desires → 0, happiness → ∞.
